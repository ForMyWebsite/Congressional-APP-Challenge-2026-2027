# Congressional App Challenge demo outline

A proposed demonstration, not a promise of scoring or impact. Check your district's actual current rules and submission requirements with the official organizer.

## Story: opportunity, with a way forward

1. **Relevance:** Talent is widespread; advising access is uneven. Show the Pennsylvania-first coverage and explain the intended grade 9–12 audience without claiming research or measured outcomes.
2. **Profile:** Optional nickname, grade/location, Engineering, free preference and a real time budget. Show skip/review and guest privacy limits.
3. **Discover:** Open a real unreviewed link and explain what remains unknown. Then explicitly switch to the **fictional** Engineering studio for the working demo. Read sample evidence, interest alignment, mismatches, missing facts and questions. Explain why this is not an eligibility score.
4. **Functionality:** Save, distinguish sample requirements from suggestions/questions, choose a student target, add schoolwork, preview sessions and approve. Show a conflict case with less time, and that unscheduled work is never hidden.
5. **Persistence:** Reload; complete/reopen steps; show dependency resets and calendar rescheduling. Export a planner and ICS calendar.
6. **Structure / code understanding:** Walk through shared Zod contracts, pure matching, topological scheduling, dependency reset and storage recovery. Each developer explains their own contribution and one edge-case test.
7. **UI:** Demonstrate keyboard use, visible focus, a phone layout, sources/availability states, clear empty states and deliberate approval controls.
8. **Potential impact and honesty:** Describe the intended benefit as a hypothesis to test with students. No fabricated testimonials, educational outcomes or guaranteed score. Close with actual remaining work: source review, established-provider sync, school agreements and privacy/consent review.

Keep the demo brief and readable. Use test data only. The implementation currently supports the guest demonstration; it does not claim a verified real program application, school import or live AI response.
